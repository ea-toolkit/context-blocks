Design Session — Payment Reconciliation Rework
2025-06-10

Attendees: Marcus Reeves, Priya Anand, Leo Chen, Nadia Volkov

We started by reviewing the current reconciliation issues. Leo walked through the three major problems with the existing Payment Engine reconciliation process.

Problem 1: Timing mismatch between ERA 835 generation and actual EFT settlement. The Payment Engine generates the remittance advice when we batch the payments, but the actual EFT settlement happens 2-3 days later. In between, things can change — a fraud hold might be placed, a claim might be voided. So the ERA 835 says we paid $X but the actual settlement amount might be different.

Marcus: this is causing real problems with provider offices. They get the 835, post it to their AR, and then the deposit doesn't match.

Problem 2: Void/reissue tracking. When the Rules Engine reprocesses a claim (e.g., due to retroactive enrollment change from Eligibility Service), the Payment Engine voids the original payment and creates a new one. But the linkage between the voided payment and the reissued payment isn't tracked cleanly. Leo showed a query where we have orphaned void records.

Problem 3: Recoupment spreading. We spread recoupments over multiple payment cycles when they exceed 50% of the payment amount. But the spreading logic doesn't account for other recoupments in flight. So you can end up with a provider getting basically no payment for 3 cycles because multiple recoupments hit at the same time.

---

Proposals discussed:

For problem 1 — Leo suggested a two-phase approach:
  - Generate a "provisional" 835 at batch time
  - Generate the "final" 835 after EFT settlement confirms
  - Providers who want real-time data get the provisional one, but they know it's subject to change
  - Priya: does the EDI 835 standard even support provisional vs final?
  - Leo: not really. We'd have to use a custom field or send two separate 835s
  - Marcus: providers hate getting two 835s. Let's just delay the 835 until settlement confirms. What's the actual delay?
  - Leo: 2-3 business days
  - Marcus: that's acceptable. Providers already wait longer for paper checks.

Decision on problem 1: delay ERA 835 generation until EFT settlement confirmation. This means we need a settlement confirmation feed from our banking partner. Leo will investigate what the bank provides.

For problem 2 — Nadia suggested adding a void_reason and linked_payment_id to the payment record:

  payments table changes:
    ADD void_reason VARCHAR(100)
    ADD linked_payment_id BIGINT REFERENCES payments(id)
    ADD void_type ENUM('REPROCESS', 'FRAUD', 'MANUAL', 'RECOUP')

  This way every void points to its replacement and vice versa. When generating reports or 835s we can show the full chain.

Marcus: what about existing voids? Do we backfill?

Nadia: we could, but it's messy. Some voids from 2024 don't have clear audit trails. I'd say we start clean — new voids get the linkage, old ones stay as-is.

Decision on problem 2: add void linkage fields. No backfill. Priya will create the migration.

For problem 3 — no decision reached. We ran out of time. The spreading logic is more complex than we thought because it also interacts with the minimum payment threshold ($5). If spreading a recoupment brings the payment below $5, do we still send it? Or do we hold? Current behavior is to send a $0 payment which is... not ideal.

Leo: we also need to think about the provider experience. A provider with $50K in monthly payments from us shouldn't be zeroed out because of two $30K recoupments hitting the same cycle.

Marcus: agreed. Let's schedule a follow-up to work through the recoupment logic specifically. Bring Tariq too — if there's a fraud hold on top of a recoupment, things get really complicated.
