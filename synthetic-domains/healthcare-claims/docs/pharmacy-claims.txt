Pharmacy Claims Processing

This page is a placeholder. Pharmacy claims are currently processed by the legacy PharmaCore system and are NOT part of the Clearview claims platform.

The plan is to bring pharmacy claims onto our pipeline as part of the 2026 roadmap. When that happens, this page should document:
  - NCPDP transaction standards (the pharmacy equivalent of EDI 837)
  - Formulary integration (covered drug lists per benefit plan)
  - PBM (Pharmacy Benefit Manager) integration — do we continue using a PBM or bring it in-house?
  - Pharmacy network and pricing (MAC, AWP, WAC — the drug pricing acronyms)
  - Clinical drug review / DUR (Drug Utilization Review) integration

For now, pharmacy claims volume shows up in quarterly metrics (~50K/quarter) but they're processed entirely outside our platform.
