METRICS (Q3 2025)

Claims volume: 2.4M claims processed (up 8% from Q2)
  - Professional: 2.0M
  - Institutional: 350K
  - Pharmacy: 50K (still routed to the legacy pharmacy system — not in our pipeline yet)

Auto-adjudication rate: 81.3% (target: 85%)
  - Marcus: we've been stuck around 81% for two quarters. The gap is mostly from rules that haven't been ported from HealthLogic yet. Priya, where are we on the migration?
  - Priya: we've ported 2,800 of ~4,200 rules. The remaining 1,400 are the complex ones — COB logic, multi-claim bundling, DRG grouper edge cases. Rachel has been helpful but she's only here until October.
  - Marcus: so we're not hitting 85% until the migration is done. Probably Q1 2026 at earliest.

Average processing time: 4.2 seconds (intake to disposition)
  - Down from 5.1 seconds in Q2 thanks to the sync fraud scoring integration (eliminated the async delay)

Denial rate: 12.4%
  - Top denial reasons:
    1. Member not eligible on date of service (3.2%)
    2. Authorization required but not found (2.8%)
    3. Duplicate claim (2.1%)
    4. Non-covered service (1.9%)
    5. Timely filing limit exceeded (1.4%)
  - Dana: the "member not eligible" denials are concerning. Some of these might be retroactive terminations that we're processing too slowly. I'll look into it.

Payment accuracy: 99.7%
  - Leo: the void/reissue tracking improvement from the June design session is working. We've reduced the orphaned void records to near zero for new payments.

Fraud holds: 1,247 claims held (0.05% of volume)
  - SIU investigated 89% within 10 business days (target: 95%)
  - Tariq: the SIU team is short-staffed. Two investigators left in August and haven't been replaced.
  - Marcus: I'll flag it to the VP. We can't let the investigation backlog grow.

    - Marcus: is there appetite for this?
