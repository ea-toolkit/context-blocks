== Escalation Matrix ==

Severity Who to page Response time
SEV-1 (full outage/data loss) On-call engineer → Marcus → Priya 15 min ack, 30 min war room
SEV-2 (degraded/partial) On-call engineer 30 min ack
SEV-3 (minor/informational) Don't page, handle next business day N/A

For SEV-1 incidents, the on-call engineer MUST:
  1. Acknowledge in PagerDuty within 15 minutes
  2. Open a Slack thread in #claims-incidents
  3. If not resolved within 30 minutes, start a war room (Zoom link in #claims-incidents channel description)
  4. Page Marcus and Priya if they haven't already joined
